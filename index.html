Koshary

To make koshari, rice, vermicelli, lentils, and macaroni are cooked separately. A tomato sauce is prepared with garlic, vinegar, and spices. Onions are fried until crispy. The dish is then assembled by placing the rice first, followed by the macaroni and lentils. It is garnished with crispy onions and tomato sauce, with the addition of boiled chickpeas and garlic vinegar dressing.

Pizza🍕

To make pizza, you need dough (flour, yeast, warm water, salt, olive oil) and sauce (tomatoes, garlic, herbs), along with your favorite toppings (mozzarella cheese, vegetables, meat). Mix the dough ingredients, knead, and let it rise. Then, roll it out, spread with sauce, cover with toppings, and bake in a preheated oven (around 180-200°C) for 10-15 minutes until cooked through and golden brown.

burger sandwich🍔

To make the burgers, mix the minced meat with the spices, form patties with a small hole in the middle to prevent them from puffing up while cooking on a hot grill until cooked, then assemble the sandwich with toasted bread, lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese, and sauces such as mayonnaise and ketchup, and serve it hot with French fries.

Pasta🍜

To make pasta, boil the pasta in salted water, then prepare the sauce (red or white) with ingredients such as garlic, onion, tomatoes or cream. Then mix the pasta with the sauce, vegetables and cheese as desired, and serve it hot. You can prepare the dough at home with flour and eggs if you want instead of using ready-made pasta.

Sushi🍣

To make sushi, you need sushi rice pickled with vinegar, sugar, and salt, nori paper, and fillings (such as cucumber, avocado, crab, or smoked salmon). Then spread the rice on the nori paper, add the fillings, roll it up using a sushi mat, cut it into small pieces after it has set, and serve it with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.

Seafood

To prepare seafood, you can use various methods such as grilling, frying, oven cooking (especially in bags), or making rich soups, focusing on a basic marinade of lemon, garlic, and spices, and using techniques such as wet cooking for lean fish or dry cooking for fatty ones, and serving it with rice or pasta.

Shawarma🌯

To prepare the shawarma, cut the chicken into thin strips and marinate it well in a mixture of yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, oil, and spices (cumin, paprika, black pepper, cardamom, coriander). Leave it in the refrigerator for several hours or overnight, then grill it in a pan with a little oil until cooked. Serve it in bread with garlic sauce and pickles.

Stuffed cabbage

To make stuffed cabbage, boil the cabbage leaves, then prepare the rice mixture (rice, chopped greens, chopped onion, garlic, tomato juice, spices, oil/ghee), then stuff the leaves, roll them tightly, and arrange them in the pot. Finally, cover them with boiling broth and cook them over low heat after it boils.

Kofta

To prepare grilled kofta, mix minced meat with chopped and drained onion, chopped parsley, spices (salt, pepper, meat spices), and garlic, then knead it well, shape it onto skewers, and grill it in the oven or on charcoal, brushing it with onion water to keep it moist, adding optional ingredients such as flour or baking powder to make it crispy.

Kabsa with chicken

To make chicken kabsa, start by sautéing the chicken pieces in oil with onions and garlic. Then add tomatoes, tomato sauce, spices (such as cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, turmeric, and kabsa spices), dried limes, and bay leaves. Let it simmer, then add boiling water and washed basmati rice. Let it cook on low heat until it absorbs the liquid and becomes fluffy. Serve it garnished with raisins and nuts.

lobster

To make delicious lobster, it can be boiled or oven-roasted with butter and garlic. Clean the lobster thoroughly, then boil it in salted water with lemon and onion for 10-15 minutes, or slit it lengthwise and marinate it with butter, garlic, parsley, and paprika, then oven-roast at 230°C for 12-15 minutes until cooked through.

American hot dogs🌭

The making of hot dogs (American sausages) involves finely grinding meat (chicken or beef) with spices (garlic, onion, paprika, nutmeg), starch, oil, and ice cubes to create an emulsion. This emulsion is then stuffed into wrappers and cooked by steaming or boiling. To prepare a hot dog sandwich, it is sautéed in a pan with oil, then placed in a baguette with ketchup, mayonnaise, pickles, and onions.

Fried eggs🍳

Heat a frying pan or add some oil until it's moderately hot. Crack the eggs and reduce the heat. Fry for 2-3 minutes on each side, or 4 minutes in a covered pan, until the whites are set and the yolks are thickened.

Taco🌮

To make tacos, prepare a filling of minced meat or chicken seasoned with Mexican spices (cumin, paprika, chili pepper) and onions, and cook it with tomatoes and corn. Then heat the tortillas and serve them with the filling, shredded cheese, lettuce, and chopped tomatoes. You can add sour cream or other sauces as desired to get a delicious and quick Mexican dish.

escalope

To make escalopes (meat or chicken), season the thin slices, then dip them in flour, then beaten egg, and finally in breadcrumbs (with Parmesan cheese optional) before quickly frying them in hot oil until golden brown. The secret is to season them well and let them rest for a bit before coating.

shrimp🍤

To prepare delicious shrimp, wash and clean them thoroughly, then sauté them in a pan with butter, oil, garlic, and spices (salt, pepper, cumin, coriander) for only 5 to 7 minutes, until they turn orange. Add lemon juice and parsley at the end to ensure they stay tender. They can also be fried by coating them in flour and egg, or baked in the oven.
