Hot drinks🥵

tea

To make tea, heat water to boiling and add tea leaves (one teaspoon per cup), then let it steep for a sufficient time (3-5 minutes for black, less for green) to get the perfect flavor. Strain or remove the leaves before serving, using filtered water and preferably avoiding reboiling the water for the best taste.

Hot chocolate

To make hot chocolate, heat the milk, then add a mixture of cocoa powder and sugar, and stir well until combined. Chocolate pieces and vanilla can be added to increase the flavor. Then serve hot. It can be thickened with a mixture of starch and milk to increase the consistency of the cream.

Latte

To make a latte, prepare a strong shot of espresso, heat the milk and make foam (by steaming or shaking it in a jar), then gradually pour the hot milk over the espresso, place a layer of foam on top, and flavors such as vanilla or cinnamon can be added, or a cold latte can be made with ice and condensed milk, according to various recipes.

Hot coffee

To make coffee, you can use instant coffee (Nescafe) by adding a spoonful of it with sugar (as desired) to hot or cold water, stirring well, then adding milk if you like; for Arabic coffee, the coffee is boiled with water and cardamom and left to rest, and for Turkish coffee, the coffee is boiled with water and sugar over low heat until foam forms.

Refreshing drinks🥶

Lemon with mint

To make a refreshing lemon and mint drink, blend lemons (sometimes with the peel), mint leaves, sugar, and water with ice in a blender. Then strain the mixture and serve it cold, garnished with mint and lemon slices. Alternatively, you can use the technique of pre-infusing mint and lemon with sugar and water to reduce bitterness and obtain a richer taste.

cocktail🍎🍓🍊🥭

To make a refreshing fruit cocktail, mix your favorite fruits (such as bananas, mangoes, strawberries, pineapples) with milk or orange juice, a little honey or sugar to taste, and some ice cubes. Then blend the ingredients well in a blender until the mixture is smooth and serve it cold. You

orange juice🍊

To make orange juice, wash the oranges well, peel them, and cut them into slices. Then blend them in a blender with a little water, ice, and sugar or honey as desired. Strain and serve it cold to enjoy it fresh. You can add boiled carrots or lemon for a different taste.

Strawberry juice🍓

To make a strawberry smoothie, wash the strawberries thoroughly, remove the leaves, then blend them in a blender with sugar or honey and water or ice, as desired. Add lemon juice or mint for extra flavor, then serve chilled. You can also add milk, yogurt, or ice cream for a richer smoothie.

Bananas juice🍌

To make a banana smoothie, cut one or two bananas, put them in a blender with a cup of milk, a little sugar or honey as desired, and you can add vanilla. Blend the ingredients well until the mixture is smooth and homogeneous, then serve immediately, with the option of adding nuts or banana pieces for decoration.

cider🍎

Place the chopped apples in a blender and add enough water to achieve the desired consistency. Blend the apples well until they form a smooth, homogeneous mixture. Then, strain the juice using a clean sieve or cheesecloth to remove any remaining peels and excess fiber. This method yields a smooth juice suitable for children.

Ice coffee

To make refreshing iced coffee at home, mix one tablespoon of instant coffee (like Nescafe) with two tablespoons of hot water and sugar to taste. Whisk well until a thick foam forms. Pour the mixture over a glass filled with ice and cold milk. You can add caramel or chocolate for extra flavor.

Pina colada

To make a piña colada, combine pineapple juice, pineapple chunks, ice, coconut milk, and heavy cream in a blender. Blend well. Taste the juice and add sugar to taste.

Boba tea🧋

To make boba (bubble tea) at home, boil tapioca pearls until soft (about 20 minutes) and then soak them in brown sugar syrup. Brew strong black tea and let it cool completely. In a glass, combine the tapioca pearls, ice, cooled tea, and milk (or cream), and stir well.

Boba balls

To make boba balls (tapioca pearls) at home, mix 1/2 cup of water with 2-3 tablespoons of brown sugar and cocoa powder (optional for color) over heat until boiling. Then add tapioca starch to form a dough. Knead enough dough, shape it into small balls, and place them in boiling water for 20 minutes. Let them rest, then dip them in brown sugar glaze.
