chocolate🍫

To make chocolate at home, mix cocoa powder with sugar, butter, and milk, then add vanilla. Gently heat the mixture until smooth and liquid. Pour it into molds and let it cool and set. Alternatively, you can melt ready-made chocolate and mix it with other ingredients as desired to create different sauces or pieces.

Strawberry jelly🍓

To make strawberry jelly dessert, dissolve a packet of ready-made strawberry jelly in a cup of hot water, then add a cup of cold water, stir the ingredients well, and you can add fresh strawberry pieces to the mixture. Then pour it into glasses and leave it in the refrigerator until it cools and freezes completely, and serve it cold.

Mango ice cream🍨🥭

To make creamy mango ice cream, mix mango puree (mashed mango) with whipped cream (or whipped Chantilly cream) and sweetened condensed milk, then add frozen mango cubes, mix well, then freeze the mixture for several hours or overnight, and you will get delicious mango ice cream suitable for hot weather.

Waffle🧇

To make fluffy, crispy waffles, whisk two eggs with half a cup of oil (or melted butter), one and a quarter cups of milk, and a teaspoon of vanilla extract. Gradually add two cups of flour, two tablespoons of sugar, one tablespoon of baking powder, and a pinch of salt, mixing until the batter is smooth and thick. Pour the batter into a preheated, greased waffle iron and cook for 3-5 minutes, or until golden brown.

Jelly Cola

To make homemade cola jelly, mix 3/4 cup of cola with 1 tablespoon of glucose syrup and 1 tablespoon of sugar, and heat until boiling. Add 2 tablespoons of gelatin dissolved in 1/2 cup of cold water, stir well until dissolved, then pour the mixture into silicone molds and refrigerate for 4-6 hours to set.

lollipops🍭

Making lollipops (candy) at home requires heating a mixture of sugar, water, and glucose syrup (or lemon juice) to a boil (approximately 145 to 150 degrees Celsius) until it turns into a clear caramel. Flavoring and coloring are then added, and the mixture is poured into greased molds with wooden sticks inserted until it cools and sets.

dumpling

To make crispy dumplings (luqmat al-qadi), mix 2 cups of flour, 1/2 cup of cornstarch, 1 tablespoon of yeast, sugar, a pinch of salt, and warm water to form a thick, runny batter. Let it rise for an hour, then use an oiled spoon to form small balls and fry them in hot, deep oil until golden brown. Finally, dip them in cold, thick syrup.
